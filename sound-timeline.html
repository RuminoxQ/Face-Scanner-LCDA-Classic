Sound timeline

Drag each sound left or right to move it. Press Play to hear the sequence. Times are in seconds from the moment the scan starts.

0.00 s

Hatched areas are not allowed: the scan sounds cannot start before the scan, and the chime, voice and logo sting cannot start before the scan is granted (the app can only delay a sound, not play it earlier). To play a sound earlier, drag the sounds that come after it to the right instead.
